# Code of conduct

Mira is a technical project, and its spaces exist to build and use it. That includes issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other channel the project runs.

## Expected

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Keep criticism about the work, specific, and actionable.
- Accept that maintainers make the final call on what gets merged.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory language or imagery
- Publishing anyone's private information
- Repeated off-topic or disruptive posting

## Enforcement

Report problems to hi@omrajguru.in. Reports are kept confidential.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, and may warn, temporarily ban, or permanently ban anyone who breaks these rules.
